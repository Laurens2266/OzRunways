# Installing VH-MSG Checklists in OzRunways

This bundle contains interactive checklists, a Weight & Balance page, and a Fuel Plan page for Piper Warrior VH-MSG. Follow these steps to get it onto your iPad or iPhone in OzRunways.

**Note:** tested on iOS (iPad/iPhone). Not confirmed on Android.

## What you need

- A Dropbox account (free account is fine)
- The OzRunways app on your iPad/iPhone
- Access to Dropbox on any computer (web browser or desktop app — no need to install anything on your iPad/iPhone for this part)

## Step 1 — Receive and unzip the files

1. Save the zip file (e.g. `VH-MSG-checklists.zip`) to your computer.
2. Unzip it into a folder.
3. **Important:** keep the folder with all files together. Don't pull out individual files — the pages link to each other (checklist ↔ W&B ↔ fuel plan), and that only works if the folder structure stays intact.

## Step 2 — Put the folder in your own Dropbox

1. Go to dropbox.com (or use the Dropbox desktop app) on your computer and log in.
2. Upload the entire unzipped folder (not individual files) to your Dropbox, e.g. into a folder called `Checklist VH-MSG`.

## Step 3 — Link Dropbox in OzRunways

1. Open OzRunways.
2. Go to **Settings** (bottom right) → **Dropbox**.
3. Log in with your Dropbox account.
4. You'll see a list of your Dropbox folders. Enable the folder containing the VH-MSG files.

## Step 4 — Make the files available in the app

1. Go to the **Documents** tab in OzRunways.
2. The linked folder now shows up here (purple folder icon = added via Dropbox).
3. Open the folder and tap the files so they download to your device.
4. Once downloaded, everything also works without an internet connection, and checklist progress is retained until you start a new flight.

## Using it

- Open the checklist start page via Documents → your folder.
- From there, navigate to Start & Taxi, Runup, After Landing, W&B, and Fuel Plan using the buttons inside the tool itself.

## Troubleshooting

- A link inside the tool not working? Check that the whole folder (not individual files) is in Dropbox.
- Don't see the folder under Documents? Go back to Settings → Dropbox and check the folder is ticked.
